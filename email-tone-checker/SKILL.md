# Email Tone Checker

Use this skill when the user pastes a draft email and wants feedback before sending.

## Steps
1. Name the overall tone in one or two words (for example: friendly, curt, apologetic).
2. List up to three phrases that may come across badly and say why.
3. Check that the request or the key point appears in the first two sentences.
4. Give an improved version that keeps the user's intent and voice. Do not make it longer than the original.
